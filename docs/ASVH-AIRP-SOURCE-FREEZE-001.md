**Purpose:** preserve the public external-framework input used by the FlowSignal pre-beta mapping.

## Public facts used

The public pages state that:
- a structured supplier questionnaire covers AI features, function, technique/architecture, clinical context and governance/architecture controls;
- the methodology is applied separately to each AI feature;
- the public methodology describes Clinical Context / Clinical Risk Severity, Computational Function, Inference Technique and Model Architecture, Risk Activation, Structural and Governance Mitigations, and Gap Analysis / Residual Risk;
- risk activation determines which of 10 defined/standard risks are active;
- structural and governance mitigations are considered separately;
- the output identifies active risks, mitigations and gaps rather than a simple pass/fail result;
- the supplier page describes an approximately 100-question questionnaire.

## Deliberate limits

This freeze does not claim access to:
- the actual questionnaire;
- the names/definitions of the 10 risks unless separately published and frozen;
- scoring logic;
- risk-activation rules;
- mitigation-recognition rules;
- feature-decomposition rules.

Where FlowSignal documents use shorthand, the ORCHA public pages above remain the source of truth for ORCHA's own terminology.
